# Teldrassil catalogue audit

Reviewed 2026-11-07. The catalogue contains **79 records**: the original 23
Shadowglen/handoff entries plus 56 additions in `Teldrassil.lua`. It is an
expanded, evidence-qualified catalogue, not a claim of exhaustive Forever
coverage or measured optimal routes. No game text, guide route, database dump
or third-party implementation was copied into the addon. Of the 56 additions,
26 are advance pickup candidates, 29 require an actual offer, and one rare
starter requires the item to be owned. Prerequisites/level still gate candidates.

## What planning can infer

Known NPC/object pickups can be proposed before interaction on map 1438, including
outside the original corridor. Unconfirmed proposals say **Check availability**.
Level/prerequisite gates apply; the actual open offer supersedes conservative
catalogue gates, while class/race, skips, completion and capacity still apply.

For most additions, the objective point is not established by the evidence.
They are **pickup-only until accepted**: the planner can group pickups at a hub,
but cannot manufacture objective/return work or unlock later quests from that
acceptance alone. After acceptance, WoW's current stage location drives work;
missing locations remain quest-log reminders. Known recipients can support
shared-return planning after work is observed. Simple evidenced deliveries have
static recipient fallbacks. Work/travel times and grey-XP reduction are estimates.

Conditional, elite, profession, regional-variant and insufficiently documented
records remain **offer-only**. Merely listing them does not assert availability.
Unknown XP defaults to a labeled estimate; unknown minimums are not asserted as
game facts and those records require actual offers. Elite/escort records retain
risk notes. No profession is inferred from level or automatically trained.

Darnassus coordinates use a different map. Rellian/Fandral and other city
handoffs have no invented Teldrassil recipient point: they wait for the client's
map 1438 projection/current stage or later map support. Other maps still pause
this catalogue.
